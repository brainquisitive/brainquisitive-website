Who we are

A learning technology company from Kolkata

BrainQuisitive Merits is a Kolkata-based MSME building AI-powered and domain-specific software products. We focus on the intersection of professional education, career growth, and smart productivity — creating tools that professionals, learners, and project managers can genuinely rely on.

Founded in 2026, the company operates under the brand BrainQuisitive and has built a portfolio of web and mobile products serving thousands of Indian professionals. Our roots are in the construction and project management domain, but our reach extends across career readiness, certification preparation, and knowledge management.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower India's professionals with intelligent, affordable tools that accelerate learning, sharpen expertise, and unlock career growth.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the most trusted companion for advanced learners across India — in classrooms, on job sites, and at every stage of a career.

💡

Our Approach

We build with deep domain knowledge first. Every product starts as a solution to a real professional problem — not a technology in search of use. We combine AI capabilities with rigorous domain expertise to ensure our tools are accurate, practical, and trusted by professionals who can't afford to get it wrong.
